English · AI My Wardrobe

Your clothes. Fresh ideas.

Organize your clothes, discover fresh combinations and explore optional AI try-on with your own provider. Three demo outfits make it easy to start.

完整介绍 / Full description

Meet your wardrobe again. Turn the clothes you already own into fresh outfit ideas. AI My Wardrobe brings a photo-first closet, everyday combinations and optional AI try-on into one clean iPhone app. YOUR CLOTHES, CLEARLY ORGANIZED Take a photo or choose one from Photos. On-device processing prepares a tidy catalog image with the goal of keeping the full garment visible. Add its name, category and color, search your collection and tap a photo to see it larger. IDEAS FOR THE DAY AHEAD Explore office, weekend and outdoor combinations. Refresh for another look and save favorites. Optional local weather adds temperature context, with units following your iPhone region. Start instantly with three demo outfits. Basic wardrobe features work without an API key. CHOOSE YOUR AI Connect your own OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, DeepSeek, Alibaba Qwen or compatible HTTPS API for conversational styling. Supported OpenAI and Google Gemini image models can create visual try-on images from your chosen clothes and model photo. Model access depends on your provider and account. YOUR REFERENCE MODEL Choose fictional male or female AI models that follow your language and region, or choose a region manually. Add optional front, left, right and back photos to use your own appearance. GET STARTED 1. Explore the demo wardrobe or add clothing in Closet. 2. Choose an occasion in Today, refresh and save a look. 3. For online AI, open Me → AI settings, enter your own key and model, review data-sharing details, explicitly allow sharing and save. 4. In Try on, choose or photograph a garment and tap AI image. Add the garment to your closet when you want. KNOW BEFORE YOU START The app is free. Online AI is optional and requires your own API key, internet access and a compatible account. Provider charges may apply, including connection tests. API access is not sold in the app. AI images are styling approximations, not a fit, size or measurement guarantee. Camera and location permissions are optional. Photos and conversations are sent to your chosen AI provider only after explicit permission, which you can revoke. Your wardrobe is stored locally, without cloud sync or app-data backup in this edition; authorized AI requests are processed by your provider. Delete individual items or all local data and keys in Me. English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French, German and Spanish. Designed for portrait use on iPhone. VOICE INPUT, YOUR CHOICE Tap the microphone to dictate in your app language. Review or edit the draft, then send when ready. Microphone and Apple speech permissions are optional. On-device recognition is used where available; otherwise Apple may process audio. The app does not save voice recordings.
